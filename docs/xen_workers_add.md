To deploy a worker and add it to the backup infrastructure, do the following:

1. [Check prerequisites and limitations](xen_workers_add_byb.md)
   .
2. [Launch the New Xen Worker wizard](xen_workers_add_launch.md)
   .
3. [Specify worker VM configuration](xen_workers_add_vm.md)
   .
4. [Specify worker network settings](xen_workers_add_network.md)
   .
5. [Finish working with wizard](xen_workers_add_summary.md)
