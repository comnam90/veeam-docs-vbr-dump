# Creating SLA-Based EC2 Backup Policies


To create an SLA-based backup policy, do the following:

1. [Launch the Add SLA-Based Policy wizard](aws_add_sla_policy_launch.md)
   .
2. [Specify a policy name and description](aws_add_sla_policy_name.md)
   .
3. [Specify a data protection scope](aws_add_sla_policy_scope.md)
   .
4. [Configure backup source settings](aws_add_sla_policy_source_settings.md)
   .
5. [Configure guest processing options](aws_add_sla_policy_guest_processing.md)
   .
6. [Specify protection settings](aws_add_sla_policy_target_settings.md)
   .
7. [Configure snapshot settings](aws_add_sla_policy_snapshot_settings.md)
   .
8. [Configure worker instance settings](aws_add_sla_policy_worker_settings.md)
   .
9. [Specify general settings for the policy](aws_add_sla_policy_retry_notification.md)
   .
10. [Review the estimated cost of protecting the selected EC2 instances](aws_add_sla_policy_cost.md)
    .
11. [Finish working with the wizard](aws_add_sla_policy_finish.md)
    .
