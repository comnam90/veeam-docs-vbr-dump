# Instant Recovery Using Console


With Instant Recovery, you can immediately restore Nutanix AHV VMs as VMware vSphere, Microsoft Hyper-V, Proxmox VE or Nutanix AHV VMs to your production environment by running them directly from their backups. Instant Recovery helps you improve recovery time objectives and minimize disruption and downtime of production workloads.
