# Creating Schedule-Based EC2 Backup Policies


To create a schedule-based backup policy, do the following:

1. [Launch the Add EC2 Policy wizard](aws_add_policy_launch.md)
   .
2. [Specify a backup policy name and description](aws_add_policy_name.md)
   .
3. [Specify a data protection scope](aws_add_policy_scope.md)
   .
4. [Configure backup source settings](aws_add_policy_source_settings.md)
   .
5. [Enable guest processing](aws_add_policy_guest_processing.md)
   .
6. [Configure backup target settings](aws_add_policy_target_settings.md)
   .
7. [Specify a schedule for the backup policy](aws_add_policy_schedule_retention.md)
   .
8. [Enable AWS tags assignment](aws_add_policy_target_settings_tags.md)
   .
9. [Configure automatic retry, health check and notification settings for the backup policy](aws_add_policy_retry_notification.md)
   .
10. [Review estimated cost of the selected EC2 instances](aws_add_policy_cost.md)
    .
11. [Finish working with the wizard](aws_add_policy_finish.md)
    .
