# Performing Instant VM Recovery


With Instant Recovery, you can immediately restore Proxmox VE VMs as VMware vSphere, Microsoft Hyper-V, Nutanix AHV or Proxmox VE VMs to your production environment by running them directly from their backups. Instant Recovery helps you improve recovery time objectives and minimize disruption and downtime of production workloads.
