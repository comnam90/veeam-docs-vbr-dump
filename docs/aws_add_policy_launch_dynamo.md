# Step 1. Launch Add DynamoDB Policy Wizard


To launch the
Add DynamoDB Policy
wizard, do the following:

1. Navigate to
   Schedule-Based

   Policies
    >
    Databases
   >
    DynamoDB
   .
2. Click
   Add
   .
