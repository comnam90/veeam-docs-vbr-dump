# Step 1. Launch RDS Restore Wizard


To launch the
RDS Restore
wizard, do the following:

1. Navigate to
   Protected Data
    >
   Databases
    >
   RDS
   .
2. Select the RDS resource you want to restore.
3. Click
   Restore
    >
   Instance Restore
   .

Alternatively, click the link in the
Restore Points
 column. Then, in the
Available Restore Points
 window, select the necessary restore point and click
Restore
.
