# Step 1. Launch Add Redshift Serverless Policy Wizard


To launch the
Add Redshift Serverless Policy
wizard, do the following:

1. Navigate to
   Schedule-Based

   Policies
    >
    Databases
   >
    Redshift Serverless
   .
2. Click
   Add
   .
