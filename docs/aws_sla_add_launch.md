# Step 1. Launch Add SLA Template Wizard


To launch the
Add SLA Template
wizard, do the following:

1. Switch to the
   Configuration
    page.

1. Navigate to
   Policy Templates
    >
    SLA
   .

1. Click
   Add
   .
